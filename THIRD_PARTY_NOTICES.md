## Gen-I Crystal battle sprites

Voxel Ascendant includes a byte-for-byte Gen-I subset of Kanto Ascendant's
documented battle-art sources:

- normal and shiny Crystal front-animation frames for Pokémon #001–151 from
  the PokeAPI sprite mirror: https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites
- normal and shiny Crystal rear cards for #001–151 from the same mirror;
- 47 normal trainer portraits derived from **Crystal Animated Sprites with
  Shiny Visuals v1.5**, pinned by the carried provenance record to commit
  `9d48cc921da4db88043cb2a14e9f8803aefffad7`:
  https://github.com/distilledorion-sketch/crystal_animated_sprites_with_shiny_visuals/releases/tag/v1.5

The upstream Crystal-v1.5 project does not publish a separate art license.
Pokémon character artwork remains copyright Nintendo / Creatures Inc. / GAME
FREAK inc. These files are included only in this unofficial, non-commercial
fan project. The exact counts and import receipt are recorded in
`assets/crystal_gen1/SOURCE.md`.
