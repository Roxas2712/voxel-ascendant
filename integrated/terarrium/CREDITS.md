# Terarrium

Die gewünschte sichtbare Design-Signatur lautet „Designed and Credits by ΩDIAS“ mit dem vom Nutzer bereitgestellten Krönchen. Die beiden Referenzbilder unter assets wurden vom Nutzer für diesen Zweck zur Verfügung gestellt.

Die Card verwendet vorhandene VASC/KASC-Pokémon und Trainerassets. Deren Urheberhinweise bleiben in den jeweiligen Basis-Paketen erhalten. Die ergänzte Bühnengeometrie, Schalensignatur, Kamerazuordnung und Ruheanimation liegen hier als separat integrierbare Mod vor.

Der BUNG-Klang wird aus einer eigenen mathematischen Klangform synthetisiert. Die Fang-Fanfare Caught_Mon wird aus den vorhandenen Spieldaten über das native Audiosystem abgespielt.

## Kanto Gym and League Terrariums

Eight compact gym interiors and badge geometry supplied by **Omega Dias / ΩDIAS (ODias)**, September 2026: Pewter, Cerulean, Vermilion, Celadon, Fuchsia, Saffron, Cinnabar and Viridian. Original Lua modules are preserved under `gyms/`, with SHA-256 source receipts. The existing ΩDIAS signature is retained on the bowl in both generation hosts. VASC integration adds bounded Fuchsia mist and Cinnabar smoke. Concept/reference images are not redistributed.

Five additional compact Gen1 League interiors and winged emblems supplied by **Omega Dias / ΩDIAS**: Lorelei, Bruno, Agatha, Lance and Champion. Original modules and source receipts are preserved alongside the gym modules. These Gen1 League interiors are not used for Johto opponents, even where map IDs overlap.

## Johto

The experimental Johto outdoor compositions were withdrawn at the maintainer's request. Existing terrain selection and scenery are retained. Dedicated Johto gym designs remain reserved for future Omega Dias contributions.

## Pokemon Tower and Rocket Hideout

Additional original compact designs by **Omega Dias / ΩDIAS**: Pokemon Tower (2F–7F) and Rocket Hideout office (B4F). The Tower ghost emblem is suppressed by the VASC adapter at the maintainer's request; original source bytes remain intact. The ΩDIAS signature remains on both bowls. Tower mist is added by VASC.

## Terrarium lighting

VASC adds independent, optional Terrarium lighting: a cached 64×64 soft contact-shadow map, two local lights on mobile and three on desktop. Cinnabar has a slow, subtle lava-light variation. Other fills are steady. No per-light real-time shadow maps or world-light discovery runs in these stages. Original ODias module geometry and palettes remain unchanged.

## Additional Kanto locations (September 2026)

Fifteen original compact designs supplied by **Omega Dias / ΩDIAS**: S.S. Anne bow, Fighting Dojo, Power Plant, Viridian Forest, Rocket Hideout B1F–B3F, occupied Silph Company, Pokémon Mansion, Safari Zone, Mt. Moon, Diglett’s Cave, Cerulean Cave, Oak’s Lab, Victory Road (Champion Road), Route 17 and Seafoam Islands. Source bytes and SHA-256 receipts are preserved under `gyms/`. VASC adds exact Gen1 routing, live Silph story gating and location-family metadata in the host adapter. Supplied documentation and concept previews are reference material, not runtime modules. The Power Plant module includes decorative Voltorb and Electrode geometry adapted from the two model archives supplied to the author; no additional model authorship or license is asserted here.
