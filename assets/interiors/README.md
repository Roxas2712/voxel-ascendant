# Kanto interior wall panoramas

Six original AI-generated wall elevations, created with OpenAI ImageGen in September 2026. PNG files retain their generated 2172 × 724 resolution.

- `lab.png`: ivory/teal research laboratory, instruments and botanical diagrams.
- `mart.png`: blue/cream retail displays and potion niches.
- `center.png`: ivory/coral/mint clinic wall.
- `home.png`: honey-oak family home with garden windows and books.
- `traditional_home.png`: timber, washi, botanical scroll and recessed ceramics.
- `coastal_home.png`: sea-blue timber, coastal pictures and brass windows.

Gen1InteriorPanoramas assigns these backdrops only to reviewed native Kanto room contracts. Existing gameplay furniture remains separate. The south and side edges remain open for the dollhouse view; these images do not add gameplay doors or change collisions. The trashed Cerulean house is excluded to preserve its story opening. Scenery Editor overrides retain priority.
